Privacy

Privacy.

Autonomy Ladder is run by Kunjar Bhaduri, doing business as Autonomy Ladder, in Dallas, Texas. This page says what this site collects, who handles it, and how long it is kept. Last updated November 25, 2026.

Data held

What this site collects

  • Email and form fields. The forms on the home page, the diagnostic, the services page, and the waitlist post to Kit (form 9506738). They ask for an email address, a role, and a firm type. The waitlist adds a plan field and a source field. The diagnostic form can include the scored tier. Kit sends a confirm link before an address is subscribed.
  • Self-score events. The self-score runs in your browser. The form records that a diagnostic was started and completed, the level claimed, and the level scored. Your individual answers are not sent anywhere unless you choose to email yourself the brief and submit the form.
  • Gap Report answers. When you take the Gap Report, your answers, the firm type and jurisdictions you choose, and the campaign tags in your link are stored on Cloudflare for 30 days so you can buy the report. If you buy it, they are kept with the report, together with the email address Stripe collects at checkout, so your report link keeps working. Your answers are not sold or shared, and we delete them on request.
  • Booking details. Intro calls are booked through Calendly, which collects your name, email address, and the meeting details needed to schedule the call.
  • Site measurement. A first-party script on this site counts page visits, clicks on key links, form submissions, checkout clicks, and the tier shown when the self-score is completed (never your answers). With each count it records the page, the referring site (its address only, never the path or query), the campaign tags in the link you followed, and whether the device is a phone, tablet or computer. Campaign tags are kept only if they match a short list of known values; anything else, such as an email address in a link, is dropped in your browser before anything is sent or stored. Recognized tags are held in your browser's session storage for that tab only, so they follow you from page to page and are cleared when the tab closes. It sets no cookies, does not store your IP address, and is not an advertising tracker; it does not follow you across other websites. The counts go to a collector we run at kunjarbhaduri.com and are deleted after 90 days. When you follow a checkout link, the same campaign tags are passed to Stripe so we can tell which page or post led to a purchase.

Checkout runs through Stripe's hosted payment page, and no payment information is collected here.

Handlers

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and receives the request data any web host receives when a page loads.
  • Kit runs the forms and sends the emails they sign up for.
  • Calendly runs the intro-call booking.
  • Stripe runs the payment page for the one paid item sold on this site and receives the payment information entered there.

Each of these services handles the information under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. Form emails are the brief and the governance notes you asked for, and every email carries an unsubscribe link that works in one click.

Retention

How long it is kept

We keep inquiry and lead information while we need it to respond and to operate. Diagnostic data is retained no longer than 24 months. Records we must keep for tax or legal reasons are kept for as long as those reasons require.
