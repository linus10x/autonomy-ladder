Three categories, and what the site does today

  • Email. The home page, the diagnostic, the services page, and the waitlist post forms to Kit form 9506738 at app.kit.com. Those forms ask for an email address. They also ask for role and firm type. The waitlist adds a plan field and a source field. The diagnostic form can include the scored tier. The waitlist page says Kit sends a confirm link.

This draft still names diagnostic answers as a category. Any copy of the individual answers kept after a form submit, and where that copy would sit, is [TBD — Kunjar/counsel].
  • Stripe customer id. This draft names a Stripe customer id as a category. The product page states there is no Stripe checkout on this site today. What would be stored if a later subscription used Stripe, and for how long, is [TBD — Kunjar/counsel].

This draft does not add customer names, testimonials, or usage counts.
