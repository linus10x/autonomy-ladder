Why

If an agent can move money, deny a claim or place an order, someone will eventually ask what it was allowed to do, who granted that, and how you take it back. "The model decided" isn't an answer an examiner accepts.

We think regulated firms should be able to run autonomous agents without giving up accountability, the audit record, or a person's final say. That's the reason this work exists.

How

The ladder runs from A0, where the agent only reads and recommends, to A4, where it runs in production with a veto it can't switch off. Each step up has to be earned.

  • Authority is granted only against evidence, and it can be revoked. The revocation is recorded too.
  • A check that the agent can't argue with sits between the agent and anything that moves money or touches a customer.
  • Every decision is written to a hash-chained record, so tampering shows up.
  • The check that clears a write is independent of the agent, so it doesn't share the agent's failure modes.

The self-score reads your program by its weakest control, not its best one. Seven questions, about two minutes, and the result stays in your browser unless you choose to email yourself the brief.

What

  • Free self-score. Seven questions, a tier from A0 to A4.
  • Six open libraries. One per regulated vertical, public, permissively licensed and archived with a Zenodo DOI.
  • Runnable controls. A funds gate that denies a transfer unless the sanctions screen finished first, a tamper-evident decision ledger, and a forwardable audit packet you can wire into CI.

These are reference implementations you can read, run and adapt. They aren't a control running in any institution's production today. Not legal advice, not a certification or audit opinion, and no guarantee of regulatory compliance.

FAQ

Short answers

What do A0 through A4 mean?

A0 Informational: the agent reads and recommends and has no write authority. A1 Assisted: the agent drafts and a human approves every write. A2 Delegated: the agent writes inside a hard envelope, with sampled review and review of every out-of-envelope decision. A3 Supervised Autonomous: in-scope writes, a non-overridable sovereign veto, and a live audit ledger. A4 Production Autonomous: A3 plus inter-agent orchestration and operator-validated escalation.

Does the self-score cost money?

No. The diagnostic is the free tier. It runs in the browser. A paid price is not charged on that page.

How is the tier chosen?

The diagnostic takes the lower of the rounded average and one step above the weakest answer, and it does not go above A4. A single low answer holds the tier down.

Can I buy a paid plan on this site today?

No. Builder and Operator are marked Plan. No price is listed and there is no checkout. The waitlist uses the same Kit form as the rest of the site.
