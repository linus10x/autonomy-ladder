These definitions are the ones already on the home page. The rung names are A0 Informational, A1 Assisted, A2 Delegated, A3 Supervised Autonomous, A4 Production Autonomous.

Levels

A0 to A4

A0
InformationalAgent reads and recommends. No write authority.
A1
AssistedAgent drafts. Human approves every write.
A2
DelegatedAgent writes inside a hard envelope. Human reviews a sampled subset and every out-of-envelope decision.
A3
Supervised AutonomousAgent writes for the in-scope decision class. Sovereign-veto layer is non-overridable, audit ledger is live. Humans supervise by exception.
A4
Production AutonomousA3 plus inter-agent orchestration and operator-validated escalation paths.

The home page states that A2 to A3 is the regulator-visible boundary. This page does not add a legal conclusion to that line.

Self-score

How the diagnostic picks a tier

The diagnostic has seven questions. Each answer is a number from 0 to 4. The tier is the lower of the rounded average and one step above the weakest answer, capped at 4. A high average does not erase a low answer.

That rule is the one in the diagnostic page script. It is a self-score, not an examination and not a certification.

FAQ

Short answers

What do A0 through A4 mean?

A0 is informational, no write authority. A1 is assisted, a human approves every write. A2 is delegated, writes stay inside a hard envelope. A3 is supervised autonomous, with a non-overridable veto and a live audit ledger. A4 is production autonomous: A3 plus inter-agent orchestration and operator-validated escalation.

Does the self-score cost money?

No. It is the free tier. Pricing marks Builder and Operator as plans.

How is the tier chosen?

Lower of the rounded average and one step above the weakest answer, never above A4.

Can I buy a paid plan on this site today?

No. There is no checkout. The waitlist uses the same Kit form as the home page.
